November 5, 1944: three waves of U.S. Navy carrier planes caught the Japanese heavy cruiser Nachi in Manila Bay and tore her apart.

Nachi had fought since the war’s first months. At the Battle of the Java Sea in February 1942, one of her torpedoes struck the Dutch cruiser Java and tore her in two.

Less than two weeks after surviving the Battle of Leyte Gulf, Nachi was in Manila Bay when the American strikes came in. Torpedoes and bombs broke her apart, and she went down about 12 nautical miles northeast of Corregidor.

Of her crew, 807 men were lost, including her captain. Only 220 survived.
